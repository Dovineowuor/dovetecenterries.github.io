Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember your actions and preferences over a period of time so you don't have to re-enter them whenever you come back or browse from one page to another.

How We Use Cookies

Dovetec Enterprises uses cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in to your account securely
  • Remember your cart while you shop
  • Understand site usage so we can improve performance and content
  • Deliver relevant ads through Google AdSense
  • Support site security and prevent fraud

Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the website to function — session management, security, and load balancing. The site cannot work properly without these.

Preference Cookies

Remember settings like language and region so the site behaves the way you expect on future visits.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting information anonymously, such as pages visited and time spent.

Advertising Cookies

Used by Google AdSense to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect site functionality — for example, staying signed in or keeping items in your cart.

  • Google AdSense opt-out & Ads Settings
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies under Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages — including Google AdSense and payment providers such as M-Pesa and PayPal. These providers are responsible for their own cookies.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
